# Record Assumptions

**Invariant:** An autonomous step resolves open choices itself and records each
choice, rejected alternative, and accepted trade-off; it never pauses to ask.

**Rules:**
- Choose the option you would recommend.
- In the affected artifact, mark `Assumption — chosen without user review`.
- Record the rejected alternative and trade-off.
- A helper's ambiguity becomes your artifact's open question or your recorded
  assumption. Never delegate asking.
- Record low-stakes deferrals as open questions.
- Count and report the artifact's assumptions.

**Check:** Is every autonomous choice auditable without reconstructing an
unmarked guess?
